# Pitch: Super Maze

## Core loop
Grab your shopping list and race through a supermarket maze before time runs out! 
Move using a virtual joystick with one thumb and collect items by approaching or touching them. Choose your route, dodge shoppers blocking the aisles, deal with falling products and power outages, and reach the checkout with everything on your list.

## Meta
Finish a level within 2 minutes to unlock the next one. Stars depend on how fast you finish: 3 stars within 40 seconds (100 coins), 2 stars within 80 seconds (60 coins), and 1 star before time runs out (20 coins). Your best rating is saved. Replaying pays out only when you beat it, and only the difference: going from 1 to 3 stars earns the missing 80 coins. A level is worth up to 100 coins in total.
Spend your coins in the shop on bonuses: 10 extra seconds, one fewer item on your list, or a protection bubble that you pop on when you need it to shrug off one bump or slip. Select up to one bonus of each type before an attempt; every selected bonus is used up when the attempt starts.
Upgrade your gear for good: better footwear makes you faster, and better lighting lets you see further during power outages. Each track has five levels, and every upgrade costs more than the last.
You have three lives across all levels. Each failed attempt costs one life, and each lost life takes a couple of minutes to recharge. Retry restarts the level from the beginning; there is no continuing a failed attempt. Out of lives? Wait for a recharge or buy a life for 100 coins, up to 3 a day.

## Why come back?
Chase three stars by returning to beat your best rating and collect the coins you missed. Save up for the next footwear or lighting upgrade that makes every future level easier, and claim your daily gift. Once a life recharges, jump back in to beat the level that stopped you and unlock the next challenge!

## Who and when
Super Maze is for players who enjoy navigating mazes and completing tasks against the clock. It is ideal for a short break, while waiting, or whenever you have a few minutes to spare. Each session lasts 1–2.5 minutes.

## Goals
- Collect an item and check it off the shopping list.
- Complete the shopping list and reach the checkout before time runs out.
- Finish a level and unlock the next one.
- Improve your completion time to earn more stars and the extra coins for a better rating.
- Earn enough coins to purchase a bonus that helps with a future attempt.
- Upgrade your footwear and lighting to their highest level.

## Feel
Follow the mini-map towards your next item, keeping an eye on the obstacles along the way. Each pickup rewards you with a satisfying sound and another check mark on your list, bringing you closer to the checkout. But bump into an obstacle and you will be dizzy for three seconds, watching precious time slip away. With your list complete, race to the checkout as the ticking sound adds pressure during those final seconds. A cash register sound marks the end of the level.

## Scope check
Three levels with shopping lists, a 2-minute base timer, and star ratings.
A coin system, a bonus shop, two permanent equipment upgrade tracks, and three lives that recharge.
Three obstacle types: blocking shoppers, falling products, and power outages.
- [ ] Hybrid-casual: simple core + a meta layer
- [ ] Portrait, one thumb
- [ ] 1–3 minute sessions
